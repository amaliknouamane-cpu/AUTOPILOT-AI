# AutoPilot AI — Demonstration Commercial Policy (Draft)

> **Status: illustrative draft for project testing only.** These terms were prepared as sample content because no official company policy document was supplied. They are not confirmed company rules and must be reviewed and approved by the business owner before being presented to real customers.

## Quotations and prices

- Prices and currency are the ones written on the quotation. The quotation is valid for 15 calendar days unless it states another validity period.
- Product availability is checked again when an order is confirmed. A quotation alone does not reserve stock.
- The final total, delivery fee, payment method, and payment due date must be shown on the quotation or invoice. Do not infer missing fees or payment details.

## Delivery and shipping

- Delivery is arranged to the address confirmed by the customer and shown on the quotation.
- For demonstration purposes, use an estimated delivery window of 3–5 business days for major Moroccan cities and 5–10 business days for other areas, starting after payment confirmation and stock confirmation.
- These windows are estimates, not guaranteed delivery dates. The confirmed delivery fee and any specific date must be stated on the quotation.
- If a delay is known, the customer should be informed and given an updated estimate.

## Returns and exchanges

- For demonstration purposes, a customer may request a return within 7 calendar days after delivery.
- The product must be unused, complete, in its original packaging, and accompanied by proof of purchase. The business must confirm eligibility before promising a refund or exchange.
- A wrong or visibly damaged delivery should be reported within 48 hours, with photographs of the product and packaging, so the business can review the case.
- Customised products, opened software, and consumables are excluded from this sample return rule unless the business owner approves an exception.

## Warranty

- Any warranty duration and coverage must match the product's manufacturer documentation and the quotation or invoice.
- Do not promise a warranty duration when it is not documented for that product. The customer should keep the proof of purchase and contact the business with the product name and issue.

## Cancellations and payment

- A customer should contact the business as soon as possible to request cancellation. Cancellation is subject to whether the order has already been prepared or dispatched.
- The payment method, bank details, and due date are only those printed on the official quotation or invoice. Never invent payment instructions.
